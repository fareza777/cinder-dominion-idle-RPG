Cinder Dominion: Idle RPG

Privacy Policy · Effective October 5, 2026

Plain-language summary

Cinder Dominion is an offline-first game. We do not require an account and we do not sell personal information. The game stores progress on your device. If you view an ad or use Google Play billing, Google may process information under its own policies.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your save, settings, accessibility choices, local ad pacing counters and purchase entitlement state in the app's private storage. This data is used to resume progress, respect your preferences and prevent duplicate local rewards. We do not upload save files to our servers.

Advertising

The Android preview uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Banner, interstitial and optional rewarded placements may process device signals such as an advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, diagnostics and ad interaction data. Google controls its processing and provides its own privacy choices. The game limits frequency and does not show ads during onboarding or active combat. You can purchase the one-time “Remove Ads” entitlement when Google Play Billing is enabled for the public release.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Cinder Dominion does not receive your full payment card number. Google provides purchase status and an entitlement token so the game can grant the purchased item. Refunds and payment settings are managed in Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and retention

We do not operate an account system, social graph or cloud save service in this release. We do not sell personal information. Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the game. Google and its partners may process data as described in their policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing. This policy may be updated when advertising, billing or online features change; the effective date above will change with each revision.
