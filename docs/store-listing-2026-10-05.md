## Full description

The last ember of Cinderwatch is fading. Choose a masked champion, set a hunt, and let the dominion move while you plan the next breakthrough.

Cinder Dominion: Idle RPG is an English-first dark fantasy incremental RPG built around purposeful progression. Every hunt feeds a larger plan: gather materials, cook provisions, forge gear, unlock a deeper route, then return to an old enemy when its rare card or masterwork material still matters.

**HUNT WITH A PLAN**
Set a target, choose your fighting style, prepare food and watch an illustrated battle unfold. Auto-combat handles the repetition while you decide what to farm, when to retreat and which upgrade changes the next fight.

**FORGE A BUILD THAT KEEPS OPENING**
Collect equipment across 21 rarity tiers, socket monster-specific cards, temper relics, grow talents and choose route bonuses. A weak enemy can stay valuable because its card, blueprint or crafting material belongs to a much stronger late-game recipe.

**A WORLD THAT REWARDS RETURNING**
Progress through Cinderwatch, regional frontiers, guardian hunts and the escalating Hollow Depths. Daily bounties, rotating merchant stock, field-journal milestones and offline reports give every return a clear next move.

**IDLE WHEN YOU WANT, ACTIVE WHEN IT MATTERS**
Leave a manual queue running while you are away, then come back to a readable report with loot, XP, crafting progress and the next useful target. Strong encounters still demand preparation; late routes are designed to keep asking for a better build.

**DARK FANTASY WITH A CLEAN SIGNAL**
Premium painted environments, sealed masks and hooded silhouettes keep the tone mysterious without obscuring the interface. Battle events, hit numbers, skill effects and status changes stay legible on a phone.

The game is free to play. The preview uses carefully paced Google test ads; optional rewarded supplies never replace manual play. A one-time Remove Ads purchase is planned for the public Google Play release. No forced account or online connection is required for the core loop.

Keep the last fire burning. Hunt deeper. Forge the impossible.
