# Action-first UI — 0.18

## Direction correction

The user found the game too narrative and unclear. Future iterations must prioritize playable systems, visible quantities, loot, costs, progress and direct actions. Do not add explanatory paragraphs or mandatory story beats to make routine gameplay clearer. Keep long help opt-in and story in the journal.

## Reference review

Reviewed the current official listing and five store images on 2026-09-26: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alexb.realmidle . The actual combat image emphasizes enemy health, supplies, combat stats and controls; the offline image emphasizes numerical results and loot. Other viewed images are promotional skill/pet/overview graphics, not a complete walkthrough. Realm Idle was not installed or played. No reference assets were copied into our game.

## Changes

- New Game enters the world directly. The cinematic remains optional in About.
- Four onboarding pages become one recipe/goal card with a direct next-task action. Existing unfinished welcome saves use their actual current objective.
- Goals shows progress and milestones with a fixed action. Farm & upgrade becomes concise resource/action cards.
- Remove repeated explanation and flavor prose from Refuge, Skills, Explore and activity preparation; preserve costs, requirements, combat mechanics, rewards and supply counts.
- Preserve portrait art. Hide empty battle panels; gate expedition shortcuts by beacon state so the early hunt list is visible sooner.
- Remove unsolicited chapter toasts; retain gameplay level-up feedback and optional story journal.
- Shorter relic and return reports, unchanged economy and save format. No new monetization.

Long-form help remains under How to play. This is a broad reduction pass, not a claim that every advanced dialog has been redesigned.
