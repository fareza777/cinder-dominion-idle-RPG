# Guardian Trials — 0.8

Three optional, repeatable encounters extend the existing regional guardians. Unlock each by clearing that region's fifth expedition tier. Existing saves gain access from their recorded kills; no reset is required.

| Trial | HP / attack / armor | Phase II | First-clear equipment | Repeat fragments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Thornbound Vigil | 480 / 20 / 10 | Bramble crush ignores 75% of armor | Epic Iron Gauntlets | 48 Ashfang |
| The Unbroken Hymn | 650 / 24 / 12 | Drowned hymn restores 8% maximum HP | Epic Iron Cuirass | 64 Emberheart |
| Crown at Sundown | 950 / 32 / 16 | Final toll uses 2.8× attack before armor | Epic Iron Shield | 96 Hollow Aegis |

Phase II begins at or below half health, stays active through enemy healing, and persists in saves. Every third enemy attack retains its regional mechanic. Stillwater and Dirge affect both phases through the same combat rules used by forecasts. Estimates conservatively assume the stronger phase throughout and are labeled as estimates.

Each first clear also awards 120 regional fragments, 15 scraps and 20 grilled minnows. These enter inventory immediately and appear in hunt reports; repeated clears do not repeat the bonus. Trial kills count toward regional field records. After all expedition tiers, Journey directs the player through the three trials.

The hall explains unlocks, both phases, build considerations, exact first and repeat rewards, and current-build risk. Its fixed footer opens preparation for one fight. Existing loadouts, workshop and hunt reports remain accessible. Combat receives a persistent phase indicator and trial-specific heading. Existing guardian portraits and arena artwork are reused intentionally.

## Production boundary

This is an adventure preview, not a certified AAA release. It adds three variants of existing guardians, not new unique animated actors. Long-term balance, player retention, device performance, bespoke animation, accessibility coverage, cloud/backend operations and store release work remain. No purchases, billing or advertising SDKs are enabled. Android export uses the existing debug signing setup; this iteration has not been installed on a physical phone.
