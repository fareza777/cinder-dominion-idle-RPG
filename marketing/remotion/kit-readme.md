# Cinder Dominion: Idle RPG — English store kit

**Campaign hook:** One more hunt.

Open `index.html` to view every asset and play both trailers. The campaign uses an iron crown, ember light, cold stone, restrained gold and readable typography. No complete human face is shown. The app icon contains only a crown and sword.

## Listing files

- `app-icon-512.png` — 512 × 512 RGBA PNG.
- `feature-graphic-1024x500.png` — 1024 × 500 RGB PNG.
- `screenshot-01.png` through `screenshot-08.png` — eight ordered 1080 × 1920 RGB PNGs.
- `trailer-landscape-1920x1080.mp4` — primary 30-second trailer with music and SFX.
- `trailer-portrait-1080x1920.mp4` — companion 30-second portrait cut.
- `trailer-thumbnail-1920x1080.jpg` — title and crown thumbnail.
- `asset-manifest.json` — English alt text, dimensions, sizes and SHA-256 checksums.
- `provenance.json` — art prompts and actual-game source details.
- `verification.json` — export dimensions, video/audio streams and file checks.
- `masters/` — original generated art for future resizing.
- `licenses/` — the two font licenses.

## Recommended screenshot order

1. **One more hunt.** — Actual dark fantasy battle.
2. **Forge your next victory.** — Duplicate fusion and 21 equipment rarities.
3. **Every enemy hides a prize.** — Monster cards and their equipment slots.
4. **Master the craft.** — Gathering and crafting within the 16-skill system.
5. **Build your own legend.** — Hero equipment, cards, talents and relics.
6. **The road gets darker.** — Locations in the Shattered Realms campaign.
7. **Go AFK. Return stronger.** — Dungeon journeys and progress while away.
8. **The end is only deeper.** — Level 130 and continued relic growth.

Screenshots use actual rendered game UI from version 0.55.1. A prepared hero reveals existing content. Crops remove some navigation while preserving the real interface. The trailer includes five seconds of animated battle and seven motion-framed screenshots. Generated citadel art supplies promotional atmosphere; it is not presented as a playable 3D scene.

The landscape trailer shows gameplay at 1.5 seconds. All copy is English and works with sound off. Music and SFX are taken from the game's original assets.

Exports follow [Google Play preview asset guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en). Play Console uses a YouTube URL for its preview video. Nothing has been uploaded or published. An editable Remotion project is supplied separately in the source archive.
